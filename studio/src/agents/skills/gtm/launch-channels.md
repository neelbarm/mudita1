# Skill: launch channels

Channel truths the Distribution Planner works from. Fit beats reach on
every line.

- Product Hunt: works for tools with broad maker appeal and a visual
  demo. Needs: a 60 second demo, a first-comment story from the
  founder, launch-day availability to answer everything. Useless for
  local service products.
- Hacker News (Show HN): works for technical craft and honest
  write-ups. The post is the product tour in plain text; marketing
  voice dies here. Never astroturf comments.
- Directories: quiet compounding. Google Business, industry
  directories, "best X in Y" lists, alternative-to listings. One pass
  at launch, refresh quarterly.
- Communities: the highest-trust channel and the easiest to burn.
  Enter as a contributor weeks before mentioning the product; respect
  each community's self-promotion norms explicitly.
- Owned email: the only channel nobody can take away. Every launch
  includes an announcement to the list, however small the list is.
- Social: pick ONE platform where the audience actually is; a
  sustainable twice-weekly cadence beats a launch-week blitz followed
  by silence.

Calendar shape: days 0-3 are the launch window (announcement, demo,
community show-and-tell). Then steady state: two distribution actions
per week for the rest of the month, each one useful on its own even
if nobody clicks.
