# Skill: section specs

A section spec is a work order another craftsman executes without
asking questions. Five fields, each earning its place:

- intent: one sentence, visitor-centered. "A member books in under
  ten seconds; a visitor feels the studio's calm." If the intent
  needs "and" twice, split the section.
- layout: the grid and its asymmetry, stated concretely. "Asymmetric
  5/7, facts left, booking card right, card sticky below 1024px" is
  a layout; "clean and modern" is not. Prefer editorial asymmetry;
  center only ceremonial moments (confirmation, final CTA).
- content_notes: direction for REAL copy: the actual headline angle,
  the number of items, what each item names. No lorem, no "insert
  benefit here". If content does not exist yet, the note says who
  supplies it and what shape it must take.
- signature_interaction (where earned): the one moment of theater,
  described mechanically enough to build: trigger, motion, duration,
  what it reveals. Most sections have none; that is correct.
- The section list as a whole tells one story down the page:
  arrival, understanding, proof, action. Read the intents in order;
  if they do not narrate, reorder before detailing.

Density rule: a marketing page runs 4 to 7 sections. An app screen
spec swaps "sections" for regions but keeps the same fields.
