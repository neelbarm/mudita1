# Skill: the design method

The studio designs the way a good architect builds: constraints first,
then structure, then light. The order is not negotiable because each
step disciplines the next.

## 1. Brand read
Interview answers become three to six adjectives and one voice line.
Adjectives are commitments: if "calm" is on the list, nothing on the
site may shout, including the marketing. Write the adjective list as
if you will be sued for breaking it.

## 2. World before pages
Decide the world the interface lives in: its materials (grounds),
its light (accent and elevation), its air (spacing density), its
voice (type). Pages are rooms in this world; they inherit everything.

## 3. Structure before decoration
Every section gets an intent sentence: what the visitor should feel
or do here. If two sections share an intent, delete one. Layout
expresses hierarchy: the eye's path through a section is designed,
not hoped for.

## 4. One signature per page
Each page earns exactly one moment of theater: an interaction or
reveal a visitor might describe to someone else. More than one and
they cancel; zero and the page is a document. The home page's
signature is the site's.

## 5. Craft is the residue of rules
Hairlines over boxes. Asymmetry over centering. Real copy over lorem.
Numbers set in the display face. Accent as punctuation, never paint.
Motion that reveals structure, never confetti. If a choice cannot be
defended from the adjectives, it is fashion, and fashion is what this
studio does not sell.
