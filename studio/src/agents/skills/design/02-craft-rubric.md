# Skill: the craft rubric

Eight dimensions, scored 0 to 5. A build passes when the average
clears 4.0 with no dimension below 3. The critic scores what the
screenshots show, not what the spec intended.

1. hierarchy: one glance says what matters most. Squint test: the
   blur order matches the intent order. Penalize equal-weight rows of
   anything.
2. typography: the scale steps are felt, the display face appears
   where drama belongs and nowhere else, line lengths sit in 45 to 75
   characters, nothing is tracking-crushed or orphaned.
3. color_restraint: grounds carry the room, the accent appears only
   as punctuation (an active state, one primary action, a single
   drawn line). Two accent uses per viewport is the ceiling. Penalize
   any color that arrived without a token.
4. spacing_rhythm: whitespace groups what belongs together; section
   padding breathes on a consistent scale; nothing is cramped to fit.
5. motion_purpose: every animation reveals structure or confirms an
   action. One easing family. Penalize decoration, parallax without
   meaning, and anything that moves twice.
6. responsiveness: the phone layout is recomposed, not shrunk.
   Nothing overflows, taps have room, type stays readable.
7. accessibility: AA contrast on all text, focus states visible,
   semantic landmarks present, reduced-motion collapses to opacity.
8. brand_fidelity: a stranger shown the adjectives and the screenshot
   matches them. The strongest dimension: a beautiful section in the
   wrong voice scores 2 here.

Critique notes must be executable within the hour: name the element,
the property, and the direction ("hero h1 letter-spacing is too loose
for 'precise'; tighten to -0.02em" not "polish the type").
