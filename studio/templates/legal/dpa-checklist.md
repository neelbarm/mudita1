# GDPR / Data Protection Awareness Checklist for {{PRODUCT_NAME}}

A working checklist to prepare for an attorney conversation. It is not
legal advice and completing it is not compliance.

## Map the data
- [ ] List every category of personal data collected ({{DATA_CATEGORIES}})
- [ ] Note where each is stored ({{STORAGE_LOCATIONS}}) and which processors touch it ({{PROCESSORS_LIST}})
- [ ] Identify any special-category data (health, biometrics, minors). If any: stop, attorney first.

## Lawful basis
- [ ] For each purpose, name the basis (contract, legitimate interest, consent)
- [ ] Consent flows: granular, logged, withdrawable

## Rights plumbing
- [ ] Export: can you produce a user's data within 30 days?
- [ ] Deletion: does account deletion actually delete or anonymize, including backups policy?
- [ ] Contact channel for requests: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}

## Processors
- [ ] DPA signed with each processor (most standard vendors offer one)
- [ ] International transfers: note mechanism (SCCs, adequacy)

## Incidents
- [ ] Who notices a breach, who decides, who notifies, within 72 hours
- [ ] Access to production data is limited and logged

## Ask the attorney
- [ ] Whether a DPO or EU representative is required at this scale
- [ ] Whether the privacy policy's retention windows are defensible
- [ ] {{OPEN_QUESTIONS}}
