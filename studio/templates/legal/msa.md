# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made between Sarga Haus ("Studio") and {{CLIENT_LEGAL_NAME}} ("Client"), effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

## 1. Structure
This Agreement governs all work between the parties. Each engagement is described in a Statement of Work ("SOW") signed by both parties. If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, the SOW controls for that engagement.

## 2. Services and standards
The Studio performs the services described in each SOW with professional skill and care. The Studio decides the technical means; the Client decides the business outcomes.

## 3. Fees
Fees are fixed per SOW. No work begins on any SOW before both signature and the initial payment described in it are received. Late amounts may pause work as described in the SOW.

## 4. Client responsibilities
The Client provides timely access, materials, and decisions. Where the Client's delay blocks the Studio for more than {{BLOCKING_DAYS}} business days, the timeline extends accordingly.

## 5. Ownership
Upon full payment for an SOW, deliverables created for the Client under it are assigned to the Client. The Studio retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, templates, and general know-how, and grants the Client a perpetual license to those as embedded in the deliverables.

## 6. Confidentiality
Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential and uses it only for this relationship. This survives termination for {{CONFIDENTIALITY_YEARS}} years.

## 7. Case studies
The Studio may reference the Client and the work publicly ONLY with the Client's prior written approval, per the Studio's published policy.

## 8. Warranties and liability
Deliverables are provided with reasonable care. EXCEPT FOR CONFIDENTIALITY BREACHES, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER AN SOW IS CAPPED AT THE FEES PAID UNDER THAT SOW, AND NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES.

## 9. Term and termination
Either party may terminate an SOW on {{TERMINATION_NOTICE_DAYS}} days written notice. The Client pays for work performed through the termination date; the Studio hands over work in progress.

## 10. General
Independent contractors; no exclusivity. Governing law: {{GOVERNING_LAW}}. Disputes go first to good-faith discussion between principals.
