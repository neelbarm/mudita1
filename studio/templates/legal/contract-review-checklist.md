# Contract Review Checklist: {{CONTRACT_NAME}}

A structured read of a third-party contract. Flags for a human and an
attorney; never advice.

## Money
- [ ] Total cost, billing cadence, and every fee that can grow on its own
- [ ] Auto-renewal terms and the cancellation window ({{RENEWAL_NOTES}})
- [ ] Late fees and suspension triggers

## Obligations
- [ ] What we must do, by when, and what happens if we slip
- [ ] Exclusivity, non-compete, or non-solicit language
- [ ] Publicity rights: can they use our name and logo?

## Ownership and data
- [ ] Who owns work product and derivatives
- [ ] License scope granted to them over our data or content
- [ ] Data processing terms and where data lives

## Risk
- [ ] Liability caps: theirs and ours; carve-outs
- [ ] Indemnities: what we would be indemnifying them for
- [ ] Termination: for convenience? for cause? what survives?
