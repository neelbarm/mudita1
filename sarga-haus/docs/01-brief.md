# Sarga Haus — Executive Brand and Product Brief

## 1. What this company is

Sarga Haus is a founder-led product studio. It takes a real opportunity — an idea,
a broken workflow, an audience with no software behind it — and turns it into a
working system: the product itself, the automation that runs it, and the pipeline
that feeds it.

The promise, in the client's language:

> Build the product. Automate the workflow. Fill the pipeline.

One partner is accountable for all three. That is the entire differentiation.
Development agencies stop at the product. Automation agencies stop at the workflow.
Lead-gen agencies never touch the product. Sarga Haus is judged on whether the
business runs.

## 2. Concept audit — weak points and how they are resolved

Before building anything, an honest audit of the concept:

1. **Five offers can read as "we do everything," which the brand explicitly forbids.**
   Resolution: the offers are not five services. They are five stages of one system,
   presented in a fixed order (Validate, Build, Automate, Pipeline, Grow). The site
   never lists them as a menu; it walks them as a sequence. A client enters at the
   stage that matches their situation.

2. **"Pipeline Sprint" risks pattern-matching to cheap lead-gen agencies.**
   Resolution: the language is infrastructure, not outcomes. We build targeting,
   enrichment, sequencing, and pipeline visibility. We never promise lead volume,
   meetings, or revenue. The section headline does this work:
   "A product without a pipeline is just a project."

3. **No real case studies at launch.**
   Resolution: the proof section is honest by design. Illustrative systems are
   labeled "Illustrative system" on every card. The site itself is positioned as
   the first proof object: the craft of the site is the demo. Case-study slots are
   structured and ready for real work (see docs/06-operations.md, workflow 14).

4. **Equity/revenue-share mention could attract tire-kickers or read as desperate.**
   Resolution: it never appears in marketing copy. Flat-fee is the stated default.
   Selective equity is a private conversation, mentioned once in the FAQ in
   commercially disciplined language.

5. **A cinematic scroll site can collapse into a slow, scroll-jacked toy.**
   Resolution: one signature moment per page, hard performance budgets, static
   fallbacks, full reduced-motion support, and no scroll hijacking. Native scroll
   only. See docs/03-motion-storyboard.md.

6. **"Sarga" will be mispronounced and its meaning is invisible.**
   Resolution: the About page carries a single quiet line on the name — "Sarga:
   the act of bringing something into form. Haus: the place where it happens.
   Said sar-gah house." Never explained anywhere else. Mystery is fine; confusion is not.

7. **Founder-led with no team can read as freelancer.**
   Resolution: the studio speaks as "Sarga Haus," not "I." The About page is a
   conviction statement, not a bio. Discipline in the offer structure (fixed
   sprints, fixed process, investment guidance) signals an institution, not a gig.

## 3. Final positioning statement

**For operators with a real opportunity and no technical partner, Sarga Haus is
the product studio that builds the product, automates the operation, and
constructs the pipeline — as one system, under one roof, at flat-fee terms.**

Category: founder-led product studio.
Not: dev shop, AI agency, lead-gen agency, freelancer.

## 4. Final message hierarchy

Level 1 — the promise (hero, everywhere):
- Build the product. Automate the workflow. Fill the pipeline.

Level 2 — the mechanism (first scroll):
- Ideas and broken operations go in. Working systems come out.
- One partner accountable for product, operations, and demand.

Level 3 — the system (mid-page):
- Five stages: Validate, Build, Automate, Pipeline, Grow.
- A named process: Clarify, Shape, Build, Launch, Improve.

Level 4 — the discipline (deep page, FAQ, services):
- Flat fees. Fixed scopes. Weekly ships. Human judgment kept in the loop.
- No promised lead volume. No fake proof. Illustrative work labeled as such.

Level 5 — the conviction (close):
- If it is real, it deserves to exist.

## 5. Voice

Declarative sentences. Concrete nouns. Verbs that describe what actually happens.
Short paragraphs. No exclamation marks, no emojis, no em dashes, no "unlock/
revolutionize/supercharge/seamless," no passion statements, no invented metrics.
When a claim needs qualification, qualify it plainly ("where it earns its place",
"labeled illustrative"). Sound like a careful operator, not a marketer.

## 6. Core customer

A nontechnical, ambitious operator: founder with an idea, creator with an
audience, consultant or coach with a manual workflow, service or agency owner
running the business out of spreadsheets, inboxes, and DMs. They buy speed,
taste, and accountability. They do not want to manage developers.

## 7. Commercial posture

- Default: flat-fee sprints with fixed scope and stated investment ranges.
- Growth Partnership: monthly retainer after a build.
- Equity or revenue share: selective, initiated by the studio, never advertised.
- The site gives investment guidance in ranges so the studio keeps pricing freedom.

## 8. Naming and brand meaning

Sarga: creation, the act of bringing something into form.
Haus: the studio — curated, deliberate, a place with a point of view.
Pronounced sar-gah house.

The mark: a square being formed — three strokes converging into a closed form.
Fragments becoming structure. It is the entire business in one glyph.
