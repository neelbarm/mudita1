## APPLICATION STRATEGY (the part that actually matters)
1. **Warm intro beats cold apply ~10:1.** Before applying anywhere, check LinkedIn for a 1st/2nd-degree connection. Use your Google advisor cohort, Mudita network, and CUNY alumni.
2. **Target ~25, not 100.** Five great tailored applications/week > fifty generic ones.
3. **For each Tier 1–2 company, tailor the lead project** (Harvey → Klauza; Clay → Styloire; Anthropic → your Claude pipeline).
4. **Track everything** in a simple sheet: company, role, contact, warm-intro path, status, follow-up date.
