# The ASAP Hiring Playbook — Neel Barmecha

Goal: signed FDE/Solutions offer as fast as possible. This is a **14-day sprint** + a steady-state
loop after. The order is deliberate — do NOT skip ahead to applying before the assets are ready.

---

## THE 3 QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

### Q: More certs?
**No. Stop collecting certs.** Ten Google AI certs is already past diminishing returns. Beyond ~3,
more certs signal "student padding a resume," not "engineer who ships." The market for FDE hires on
**shipped products + ability to talk to customers**, neither of which a cert proves. Spend zero more
hours here. (Only exception worth considering later: nothing. You're done. Build instead.)

### Q: Specific projects to build?
**Yes — but only "company-cased" demos (see Phase 3).** You already have 5 products proving you can
ship. You do NOT need a 6th generic product. You need 1–2 demos that mirror *what a specific target
company does*, so the hiring manager sees you doing their job before you're hired.

### Q: Case companies?
**Yes — this is the highest-leverage tactic in the whole search and ~95% of applicants never do it.**
FDE roles are about deploying a product at a customer. So the ultimate proof is: pick a target company,
build a tiny working demo of their product solving a real customer problem (or a 1-page "how I'd deploy
you at customer X" teardown), and send it with your application. It converts cold applications into
"we need to talk to this person."

---

## PHASE 0 — POSITIONING (Day 1, ~2 hours). Do this before anything else.
Nothing else works until this is fixed.
- [ ] Pick **one identity**: "Forward-Deployed AI Engineer." Everything else = supporting evidence.
- [ ] Write the **flight-risk one-liner** (why you won't leave for med school / your own startup).
      Put it in resume summary + LinkedIn About. This is the #1 thing silently killing your profile.
- [ ] Rewrite your **LinkedIn headline**: `Forward-Deployed AI Engineer · ships production AI in <2 weeks · Claude API / RAG / Next.js`
- [ ] Update LinkedIn **About** with the new positioning + flight-risk answer + 3 proof bullets.

## PHASE 1 — ASSETS (Days 1–3)
- [ ] **One-page resume** from the bullets file (FDE order: Styloire → Klauza → Common Collective → Spur).
- [ ] **Pin & polish 3 GitHub repos** with deployment-story READMEs (use the Styloire template). Make
      Spur and Klauza public and pinned. A hiring manager WILL click your GitHub — make the top 3 sing.
- [ ] **30–60s Loom/screen-recording** for each of your top 2 products. A working demo video > any bullet.
- [ ] **Tracking sheet**: company | role | contact | warm-intro path | status | next-follow-up-date.

## PHASE 2 — PIPELINE (Days 3–5)
- [ ] Build the **25-company target list** (use the target file; flag NYC + warm-intro paths).
- [ ] For each, find **a real human** (FDE, eng lead, or recruiter) on LinkedIn — not just the job post.
- [ ] Map **warm-intro paths**: Google Youth Advisor cohort, Mudita network, CUNY alumni, anyone 2nd-degree.
- [ ] Set up **saved job searches** (LinkedIn + YC Work at a Startup + Wellfound) for the exact titles.

## PHASE 3 — THE CASED-COMPANY DEMOS (Days 5–10) ← your differentiator
Pick your **top 3 companies** (suggest: Harvey, Anthropic, + one NYC Tier-2 like Ramp/Hebbia/Clay).
For EACH, ship ONE of these (a weekend each — your exact speed):

**Option A — Mini working demo of their product**
e.g. for **Harvey**: a small legal-doc analyzer (you basically already built this as Klauza — reframe
+ sharpen it toward enterprise contracts). For **Clay**: a GTM-enrichment + personalized-outreach flow
(Styloire is 70% of this). For **Anthropic**: a clean agentic workflow showcasing MCP + tool use.
→ The pitch writes itself: "I built a slice of what you do, over a weekend. Imagine with your team + data."

**Option B — Deployment teardown (1 page)**
"How I'd deploy [Company]'s product at [specific customer segment]" — the workflow, the integration
points, the risks, the first-week plan. This is *literally the FDE interview case*, done unprompted.

Ship at least **2 demos / teardowns.** These become your outreach hook (see template #5 in outreach file).

## PHASE 4 — OUTREACH AT VOLUME (Days 8–14, then ongoing)
Daily quota (do these every weekday — consistency beats bursts):
- [ ] **5 warm-intro asks or cold DMs** (lead with a demo or a shipped product, never your title).
- [ ] **3–5 tailored applications** (tailor the lead project to the company; attach the cased demo for top targets).
- [ ] **All follow-ups due that day** (4-business-day bump cadence).
Target volume to land an FDE role fast: roughly **40–60 quality touches** in motion at once. ~25 great
tailored applications + warm intros will typically generate 3–6 first conversations.

## PHASE 5 — INTERVIEW PREP (in parallel, Days 7+)
FDE loops are NOT LeetCode marathons. Expect:
1. **Practical coding** (medium, real-world: parse this, integrate that, build a small endpoint). Stay
   sharp in Python — practice building a small API + an LLM tool-call live.
2. **Systems / integration design** ("design how we'd ingest a customer's data and serve it"). Practice
   talking through APIs, data flow, auth, failure modes.
3. **Customer case round** ("a customer says X — walk me through your first week"). YOU WIN HERE as a
   founder. Practice out loud: clarify the goal → scope → de-risk → ship something small fast → iterate.
4. **Behavioral / "why us, why this, why not med school / your own startup."** Have the crisp answer.
Prep: do 3–4 mock runs of the customer-case round out loud. It's the round that separates FDEs from SWEs
and it's your strength — don't wing it, rehearse it.

---

## THE WEEKLY LOOP (until signed)
Mon–Fri: 5 outreach + 3–5 tailored applies + all follow-ups. 1 cased demo per week for a fresh top target.
Fri: review the tracker — what's stalled, who to bump, which message is converting, double down on it.

## WHAT NOT TO DO (time sinks that feel productive)
- ❌ More certifications.
- ❌ Another brand-new product nobody asked for.
- ❌ Mass-applying with the same generic resume (low conversion, burns the funnel).
- ❌ Endlessly polishing your personal site instead of talking to humans.
- ❌ Leading with "pre-med EMT founder doing all three" — pick one identity per application.

## THE 80/20
If you do only three things: (1) fix positioning + flight-risk line, (2) ship 2 cased-company demos,
(3) send 5 warm/cold touches every weekday. That alone gets you interviews within ~2 weeks.
