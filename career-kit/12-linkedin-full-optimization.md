# LINKEDIN — FULL OPTIMIZATION, FIELD BY FIELD
*Paste-ready. Work top to bottom; the whole thing is ~35 minutes. Priority order is deliberate — items 1–4 do 80% of the work.*

**How LinkedIn actually gets you hired:** recruiters don't browse, they run keyword searches inside LinkedIn Recruiter and filter by title, skills, and location. Your profile's job is (a) to *appear* in those searches and (b) to survive the 6-second skim once it does. Every choice below serves one of those two jobs.

---

## 1. HEADLINE — the single highest-leverage field
It's indexed heavily in recruiter search, and it follows you into every comment, DM, and search result. 220 characters.

**Use this (primary):**
```
Forward-Deployed AI Engineer · Ship production AI in <2 weeks · Claude API, MCP, RAG, Next.js · 5 products live · Google Global Youth AI Advisor · NYC
```

**Variant if you lean GTM/automation track (file 6, track 1–2):**
```
AI Engineer · GTM & Automation Systems · Claude API, MCP, RAG, n8n, Next.js · Shipped 5 production products solo · Google Global Youth AI Advisor · NYC
```

**Why it's built this way:** it front-loads the exact title recruiters type ("Forward Deployed Engineer," "AI Engineer"), then the differentiator (velocity), then searchable stack keywords, then credibility, then location. No adjectives, no "passionate about."

**Do not use:** "Pre-med EMT, AI builder, founder — doing all three at once." It's a great personal brand and a bad recruiter-search string. It matches zero queries and reads as flight risk. One identity per profile.

## 7. THE PART EVERYONE SKIPS — activity
A polished profile with zero activity converts far worse than a good profile with visible momentum. LinkedIn shows recruiters your recent activity, and comments reach further than posts when your network is small.

**Weekly, 20 min/day:**
- **3 substantive comments/day** on posts by people at your target companies (Anthropic, Ramp, Decagon, Distyl, Clay). Not "Great post!" — one specific technical observation. This is the single fastest way onto a hiring manager's radar, and it makes your later DM warm instead of cold.
- **1 post/week** from file 11 (the build-in-public drafts).
- **Connection requests:** 10–15/day to FDEs, AI engineers, and recruiters at target companies. Always with a note, always under 300 characters:
```
Hi [Name] — I build forward-deployed AI products in NYC (Claude API, MCP, RAG; five shipped solo). Following [Company]'s work closely and would love to connect with people doing this for real.
```

**Order of operations that works:** connect → engage with their content for a few days → *then* send the DM from file 8. Cold DMs to strangers convert at a few percent; DMs to someone who's seen your name on three thoughtful comments convert far better.

## 8. THE 6-SECOND TEST
When you're done, open your profile on your phone, signed out. In six seconds a stranger should get:
1. **What you are** — Forward-Deployed AI Engineer (headline)
2. **Proof** — five shipped products, one with a number (About, first lines)
3. **That you're available** — Open to Work + the closing line

If any of the three isn't obvious in six seconds, fix that field before touching anything else.
