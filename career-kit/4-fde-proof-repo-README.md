# Styloire

**One personalized outreach email to every brand on a stylist's list — sent instantly.**
Built end-to-end for a fashion-tech founder. From first conversation to production MVP in **under two weeks**.

🔗 Live: [styloire.co] · 👤 Built for: Jessica Mastorakis (founder)

## The customer & the problem
Fashion stylists pitch brands for collaborations one email at a time — researching each brand,
rewriting the same pitch, and tracking replies by hand. For a stylist with a list of 100+ brands,
that's hours of copy-paste per campaign and pitches that read like spam.

**The ask:** "Make it so I can send a genuinely personalized pitch to every brand on my list at once."

## Constraints (the real FDE part)
- **One customer, real workflow** — had to fit how *she* actually works, not a generic tool.
- **Speed** — needed something usable in days, iterating on live feedback.
- **Personalization at scale** — 133+ brands per send, each email specific enough to land.
- **Solo build** — discovery, architecture, build, deploy, all me.
