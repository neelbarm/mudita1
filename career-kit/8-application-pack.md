## UNIVERSAL PIECES (memorize these — every company will ask)

### The 90-second "tell me about yourself"
> "I'm a builder in New York. I run a one-person studio where I've shipped five production products — my cadence is MVP in a week, production in two. The one that best explains what I do: a fashion founder needed her stylists to stop hand-writing brand pitches, so I embedded with her, scoped the real workflow, and shipped her entire platform end-to-end in under two weeks — it sends one personalized email to every brand on a stylist's list. I've also built an AI contract scanner on the Claude API, and an agent that plans and actually books a full NYC day. Before software, I put in 2,000+ hours as an EMT, which is where I learned the thing I think matters most in this job: walk into chaos, find the real problem, act fast, stay human. I'm here because I've proven I can ship alone — now I want real customers and stakes bigger than I can reach solo."

### The commitment answer (they WILL probe pre-med + founder)
> "I've made my choice — I build. The ER taught me I'm at my best shipping tools for humans under pressure, and this role is exactly that. And I'm not shopping for my next startup idea; running a studio taught me my ceiling alone. I want to be inside a team where the deployments are bigger than anything I can reach solo."

### Salary expectations (say this, not a number first)
> "I'm focused on fit — I know your band for this role is public/competitive. My expectation is base above $[120–140]k depending on the total package, and I care more about ownership and velocity than squeezing the top of the band."
*(Calibrate the floor per company: Ramp/Distyl ~$130k+, Anthropic/Glean/Decagon ~$150k+. Never open with a number if they'll say theirs first.)*

### Rules for every application
- Attach/link: portfolio site, the specific proof repo for that company, and (when asked "anything else?") the cover note below.
- Application answers: short paragraphs, concrete nouns, one number per claim. No adjectives without evidence.
- Same-day follow-up: find one human at the company (FDE, eng lead, or recruiter) and send the DM.
