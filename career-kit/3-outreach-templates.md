## 0. THE OBJECTION TO PRE-EMPT (read first)

Every recruiter looking at your profile silently asks:
**"He's a pre-med EMT AND a founder — won't he leave for med school or his own startup in 6 months?"**

You must answer this *before* they ask. Your one-liner (adjust to what's true):

> "I've decided I want to build AI products that reach real people, not practice medicine — the EMT work taught me I'm at my best shipping tools for humans under pressure, and FDE is exactly that job. I'm not looking to start another company right now; I want to do this inside a team building something bigger than I can alone."

Put a version of this in your resume summary, your LinkedIn About, and have it ready for interviews. **This single sentence is probably the highest-leverage edit in your whole search.**
